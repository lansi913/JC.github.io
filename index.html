About Me ☺️

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in information and communication engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Changzhan Gu. My research interests are mainly in millimetre-wave radar systems and artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms in microwave sensing, especially in biomedicine, human-computer interaction (HCI) and indoor localization.

I am concerned with the construction of machine learning models with good interpretability and high robustness, including custom pattern recognition models dominated by kernel method and Bayesian inference, and the construction of multimodal big models driven by neural networks. More generally, I have broad research interests in smart sensing hardware, interpretable physiological signal processing and pattern recognition, as well as general AI models developed based on multimodal data and the “scaling law.”

I have had 15+ papers accepted for publication, including three first-author journal papers in in IEEE TMTT / EMScience, and I have two granted Chinese patent applications. The first is “A Millimeter-Wave-Based Method and System for Concentration Evaluation, and the second is “A Radio Frequency-Based Monitoring System for Children Mouth Breathing.” As a presenter, I have also been recognized with the Best Student Paper Award at both the 2025 IEEE IWS and IMWS-AMP conferences.

I am a reviewer for the IEEE flagship journals TMTT / JBHI / TIM, and I was recognized as an Outstanding Reviewer for TIM in 2025. During my undergraduate studies, I served as a president of the student union at my campus and gained extensive experience in organizing student activities. I enjoy writing, photography, music, and running in my free time.
